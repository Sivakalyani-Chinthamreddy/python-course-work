#polymorphisam (means many forms with same method)
--Polymorphism means one method can have multiple forms or 
different behaviors depending on the object.
--Polymorphism is the ability of the same method or operation to perform 
different behaviors depending on the object.

#polymorphism has mainly 4 types:
--Method overriding:Method overriding is a feature where a child class provides
 a different implementation of a method that is already defined in its parent class.
